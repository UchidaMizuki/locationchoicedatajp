# locationchoicedatajp 0.0.0.9000

* Initial package infrastructure (#2).
* `census_city_centroid_get()` returns the population centroids of municipalities published by the Statistics Bureau for 2015 and 2020 as `sf` points, the representative points for distances, and `census_city_centroid_target()` declares them as a target (#8).
* `census_commuting_get()` returns the flows of employed persons or students between municipalities from the Population Census of 2020, by sex and place of work or school (at home, outside the home, another municipality, unknown), with zero flows kept, and `census_commuting_target()` declares them as a target (#6).
* `census_grid_get()` returns the grid square statistics of the Population Census (2010, 2015, and 2020; 1 km, 500 m, and 250 m) as published, with their suppression, `census_grid_tables()` and `census_grid_items()` list their tables and items, and `census_grid_target()` declares them as a target (#7).
* `data_sources()` lists the source, publisher, and terms of use of every getter (for National Land Numerical Information, the terms of the releases it uses, which differ by release), and `data_attribution()` gives the lines to credit the sources of the getters used; every getter shows its terms from this registry (#4).
* `grid_city_get()` returns the land area and share of each grid square in each municipality, from the administrative boundaries in force on a date, and `grid_city_target()` declares it as a target (#5).
* `grid_code()` gives points, such as the sites of `land_price_get()`, the code of their grid square (#5).
* `grid_sizes()` lists the sizes of grid squares (1 km, 500 m, and 250 m), which every function on grid squares takes without a default. Each size is built on its first call without rebuilding the others (#5).
* `land_price_get()` returns the sites of the official land prices (地価公示) with their location as an `sf` tibble for every year from 2007, each year from its own release, and `land_price_target()` declares them as a target (#9).
* `land_price_grid_get()` summarises the sites of the land prices by grid square and use (number of sites, median, and geometric mean), and `land_price_grid_target()` declares it as a target (#9).
* `medical_institution_get()` returns the hospitals, clinics, and dental clinics of National Land Numerical Information (医療機関, P04) for 2020 as `sf` points, as published, and `medical_institution_target()` declares them as a target (#10).
* `prefectural_land_price_get()` returns the sites of the prefectural land price survey (都道府県地価調査) with their location as an `sf` tibble for every year from 2006, each year from its own release, with forest land converted to yen per square meter, and `prefectural_land_price_target()` declares them as a target (#9).
* `rail_line_get()` returns the railway lines of National Land Numerical Information (鉄道, N02) for 2006 to 2008 and 2011 onward as `sf` lines, one row per section, as published, and `rail_line_target()` declares them as a target (#10).
* `rail_station_get()` returns the railway stations of National Land Numerical Information (鉄道, N02) for 2006 to 2008 and 2011 onward as `sf` lines, as published, and `rail_station_target()` declares them as a target (#10).
* `school_get()` returns the schools of National Land Numerical Information (学校, P29) for 2021 and 2023 as `sf` points, as published, and `school_target()` declares them as a target (#10).
